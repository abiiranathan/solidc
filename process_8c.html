Detailed Description

Portable (platform-neutral) process management logic.

Hosts everything that does not touch OS APIs. Platform-specific process creation, pipes, waiting, and signaling live in:

  • src/process/process_posix.c (Linux/macOS/BSD)
  • src/process/process_win32.c (Win32)

The contract between the two sides is defined in process_internal.h.
